*   *   Rather than focusing on *after* the workout, prioritize having enough protein *before* the workout (30 minutes before).
* **Food Sources:**
* You can get enough protein from real food sources such as chicken or fish
* For pre-workout a banana or sweet potato is good.
* (BCAA have also been marketed, but are not as needed).
* **Key takeaway:**
* Focus on getting enough protein through the regular whole food you eat, rather than chasing magic ratios or supplements.
* It's all about what works for you.
